# Governance

GovAI-NZ is an independent community project. The initial repository owner maintains scope, approves releases, and resolves contribution disputes. Participation is open to contributors using any development tooling.

Public technical decisions belong in issues or architecture decision records. Changes to audit contracts, information boundaries, provider permissions, or human-review behaviour require explicit maintainer review. Add independent reviewers as the community grows; do not describe a sole-maintainer project as having two-person assurance.

The project cannot approve systems for government use. Agencies retain responsibility for their own assessment, procurement, privacy, security, records, and deployment decisions. No party affiliation or government endorsement is implied.
